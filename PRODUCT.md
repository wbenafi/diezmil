## Platform
web
## Stack
Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, localStorage. User requested Next.js and local persistence.
## Users and purpose
Groups sharing one phone while playing 10.000 with physical dice. Record manually calculated scores with very few taps.
## Capabilities and constraints
The authoritative feature brief is PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md. Spanish interface. Player names and reorderable turn order; manual points and +50/+100/+500; save and automatic turn advance; undo including a winning turn; turn history. Tapping an entire scoreboard row opens that player's compact turn-history modal, with player-specific numbering, running totals, and the highlighted scoring turn at risk of a penalty. Setup includes three independent optional rules, enabled by default for new games: exact 10,000 (going over records a scoreless bust), a penalty that removes the latest eligible scoring annotation after three consecutive turns without adding points and then resets that counter to zero, and a tie penalty. All three rules can be disabled individually. With the tie penalty enabled, when a player adds points to match another player’s total, the matched player loses their latest eligible positive scoring annotation. All opponents tied at that total are affected simultaneously; deductions do not cascade. Zero-point turns, busts, and score losses do not trigger ties. Both penalties share annotation eligibility so a scoring annotation cannot be deducted twice and totals cannot become negative. An incoming tie penalty neither counts as the recipient’s turn nor resets their scoreless streak. The three-dot menu allows changing any rule during play; changes affect upcoming turns, preserve past scores, and activating the three-turn penalty starts a fresh streak. Zero-point turns advance and are recorded (confirmed by user), and the live standings show scoreless streaks only while their rule is active.
Preview tie deductions before saving; show their effects in the last turn, standings, global history, and recipient’s history with running totals. Undo restores the scoring turn and every affected total together. Persist setup, rules, names, order, totals, active player, input, history, and finished status in this browser; existing saved games migrate with the tie rule disabled. No accounts, backend, digital dice rolling, statistics, or scoring configuration beyond these three rules.

## Product principles
Keep scores visible, emphasize the current player, avoid confirmations except when replacing an active game, and recover quickly from mistaken entries.

"Penalties at 300 or more" is a game rule, on by default for new games: the three-turn and tie penalties wait until every player's running total has reached 300. Points below 300 always count. While penalties wait, the standings show one global notice. The turn that brings the last player to 300 is protected; rayitas start at zero after it. Reaching 300 is derived from saved history, so undo/reload work and later deductions below 300 do not pause penalties again. Adding a player during play pauses penalties until they reach 300. Turning the rule off applies penalties immediately with a fresh streak. Games saved before this rule existed keep it off, so their results are preserved.
